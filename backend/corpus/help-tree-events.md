**Key takeaway:** TreeBaltimore keeps a calendar of tree events across the city. It is the easiest place to find a planting to volunteer at or a class to attend.

## Kinds of events you will find
- **Trainings and classes**, such as the TreeKeepers Introduction to Planting class and hands-on field sessions.
- **Community tree plantings** and site preparation days in specific neighborhoods, often run with partner groups like Blue Water Baltimore.
- **Information sessions** on forestry topics, such as deer management.
- **Social gatherings** for people who care about trees, such as a summer get-together.

Free yard tree adoption pickup events are listed separately at https://www.treebaltimore.org/shop.

## How to use the calendar
1. Go to https://www.treebaltimore.org/treeevents.
2. Pick an event and follow its details to register or show up.
3. If your group is hosting a tree event, the page has a form to submit it to the calendar.

Specific dates change every season, so always check the calendar for what is coming up.

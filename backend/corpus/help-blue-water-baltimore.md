**Key takeaway:** Blue Water Baltimore is a nonprofit working for clean water in Baltimore. It plants street trees, which it says help reduce flooding by slowing rainwater, and it offers volunteer opportunities year-round.

## Ways to volunteer
- **Tree planting and pruning** around Baltimore
- **Stream cleanups** to pull trash out of local waterways
- **Herring Run Nursery** help at their native plant nursery (https://herringrunnursery.bluewaterbaltimore.org)
- **Advocacy**, such as letter-writing parties for environmental policy
- **Office help** with administrative tasks

## How to sign up
- **Individuals:** check the events calendar at https://bluewaterbaltimore.org/events/ and register for a volunteer day.
- **Groups:** community groups can email volunteer@bluewaterbaltimore.org. Businesses can sponsor volunteer events (sponsorships start at $500) by emailing mjenkins@bluewaterbaltimore.org.
- If you need accommodations to take part, contact volunteer@bluewaterbaltimore.org or call (410) 254-1577 x121.

## Street trees from Blue Water Baltimore
Blue Water Baltimore's street tree FAQ (https://bluewaterbaltimore.org/treefaq/) says it plants trees in the strip between the sidewalk and curb, maintains them for their first 2 years, and then the city's Forestry Division takes over. To ask about a street tree, it lists trees@bluewaterbaltimore.org. It asks residents to help water new trees, about 20 gallons a week, slowly.

## Donate
Blue Water Baltimore accepts one-time and recurring gifts at https://bluewaterbaltimore.org/donate/.

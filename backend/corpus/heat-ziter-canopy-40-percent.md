---
title: "Why 40% tree canopy matters: block-scale cooling (Ziter et al. 2019)"

source: Ziter et al., PNAS 2019
---

**Key takeaway:** Trees cool daytime air the most once tree canopy covers more than about 40% of the area, and the cooling is strongest when you look at an area the size of a typical city block (about 60–90 meters around a spot). Below that level, adding trees still helps, but the effect is smaller.

## The study
Carly Ziter, Eric Pedersen, Christopher Kucharik and Monica Turner measured air temperature by bicycle in Madison, Wisconsin, a midsized city in the Upper Midwest, during summer 2016 (May 30 to September 6). A sensor on the bike recorded air temperature every 5 meters along 10 routes of roughly 7 km each. The routes crossed places with everything from no trees to full tree cover, and from no pavement to all pavement. The paper was published in the Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences (PNAS).

## What they found
- **Big differences within the city:** Daytime air temperature along a route varied by an average of **3.5°C** (6.3°F), with a range of 1.1–5.7°C.
- **The 40% threshold:** Temperature dropped in a curve, not a straight line, as canopy increased. The greatest cooling came when canopy cover was **above 40%**.
- **Block scale:** Cooling grew stronger as the researchers looked at larger circles around each point, and was greatest at **60–90 m**, about one city block.
- **Hottest days:** The cooling benefit of more canopy was largest on the hottest days, when air temperature was above **30°C** (86°F).
- **Pavement:** More paved surface made days warmer, but the warming from pavement was smaller than the cooling from trees.
- **Night:** At night, temperature differences averaged **2.1°C** and trees had limited effect. Pavement still raised night temperatures, so reducing paved area matters for cooler nights.

## What it means for planting
A few scattered trees may not be enough to make a block noticeably cooler. Concentrating plantings so a whole block gets close to or above 40% canopy can bring bigger cooling. The authors also point out that tree cover tends to be higher in wealthier areas, so planting decisions should consider fairness, and removing pavement helps with nighttime heat.

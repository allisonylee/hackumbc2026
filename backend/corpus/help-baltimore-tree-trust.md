**Key takeaway:** Baltimore Tree Trust is a nonprofit that plants and cares for street trees. You can volunteer at their plantings in spring and fall with no experience needed; they provide tools and training.

## About the group
According to its Our Story page (https://www.baltimoretreetrust.org/who-we-are/our-story/), Baltimore Tree Trust was organized in 2008 by six founders, and its first plantings were in East Baltimore's McElderry Park. It has planted over 21,000 trees citywide and works toward the city's 40% canopy goal. It provides two-year warranty care for newly planted trees, and it runs a nursery in Park Heights.

## What volunteers do
- Planting days: digging, planting and mulching, mostly in the **spring and fall** planting seasons.
- Tree care: watering routes, mulching, and tree health checks.

## Who can volunteer
- Individuals and community members
- Groups such as neighborhood associations, faith communities, youth groups and scouts
- Corporate teams (custom team-building days)

No experience is needed. Just bring energy and be ready to get your hands dirty.

## How to sign up
1. Go to https://www.baltimoretreetrust.org/ways-to-take-action/volunteer/.
2. Fill out the volunteer interest form (name, email, phone, group type, and how many people).
3. The team will follow up about upcoming events.

## Want trees in your neighborhood?
Baltimore Tree Trust's FAQ page (https://www.baltimoretreetrust.org/resources/faqs/) says it reaches out to neighborhoods through local groups, mailers and door-knocking before a project. For a city street tree, you can also use TreeBaltimore's form: https://www.treebaltimore.org/street-tree-request-form.

**Key takeaway:** Trees remove a lot of air pollution in total, but they only improve air quality a little in any one place (usually less than 1 percent). The health value is concentrated in cities, because that is where people breathe the air that trees clean.

## How trees remove pollution
- **Particles** (such as fine particulate matter, PM2.5) stick to leaf and branch surfaces.
- **Gases** such as ozone (O3), nitrogen dioxide (NO2) and sulfur dioxide (SO2) are taken in mostly through leaf pores (stomata).

Trees can also affect air quality indirectly by cooling the air and cutting building energy use. Some species also give off volatile organic compounds that can help form ozone. The authors note that trees, especially low-emitting species, can still help lower urban ozone.

## National results (2010)
US Forest Service researchers modeled every county in the lower 48 states:
- Trees and forests removed about **17.4 million tonnes** of air pollution in 2010 (range 9.0 to 23.2 million tonnes).
- The health benefits were valued at about **$6.8 billion** (range $1.5 to $13.0 billion).
- The benefits included avoiding more than 850 deaths and about 670,000 cases of acute respiratory symptoms.
- The average improvement in air quality was **less than 1 percent**. By pollutant, it ranged from 0.13 percent (PM2.5 in urban areas) to 0.51 percent (ozone in rural areas).

## Why urban trees matter most for health
Most of the pollution was removed in rural areas (16.7 million tonnes, versus 651,000 tonnes in urban areas), because most land is rural. But 68.1 percent of the health value ($4.7 billion) came from urban trees, because they grow close to people. Per hectare of tree cover, the value averaged about $481 in urban areas versus $9 in rural areas. The greatest values were in the most densely populated places. In general, more tree cover and more people meant more health value.

## Caveats
These are computer model estimates. The authors note uncertainties in tree cover, pollution data and health modeling, so the true range may be wider than reported.

**Key takeaway:** Baltimore City residents can get free young trees to plant in their own front or back yard through TreeBaltimore's yard tree adoption events, held every spring and fall.

## What you get
- Trees are **free** ($0.00).
- They are small young trees, roughly 12 to 36 inches tall, in 1 to 3 gallon pots.
- You can reserve **up to 3 trees per household**, with a limit of one per species.
- These trees are for **private yards only**, not for sidewalk tree pits or strips. For a sidewalk tree, use the street tree request form instead.

## How to get one
1. Go to https://www.treebaltimore.org/shop.
2. Look at the map and list of upcoming pickup events.
3. Pre-register by choosing your trees and a pickup event. Walk-ups may also be possible.
4. Put the date on your calendar and pick up your trees. Tell your neighbors so they can get trees too.

The page lists each season's pickup events with dates and locations, so check it for current details.

## Host a giveaway
TreeBaltimore's "get a free tree" page (https://www.treebaltimore.org/get-a-free-tree) says you can host your own yard tree giveaway for your neighborhood. Its contact link goes to treebaltimore@baltimorecity.gov.

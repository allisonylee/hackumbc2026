**Key takeaway:** TreeBaltimore is Baltimore City's tree-planting partnership. It is a mayoral initiative run by the Forestry Division of the Department of Recreation and Parks, and it works to grow the city's tree canopy to 40%.

## What TreeBaltimore does
TreeBaltimore serves as the umbrella organization for city agencies, private organizations and individual tree stewards involved in growing the canopy. It offers several ways for residents to get involved.

## Main things you can do through TreeBaltimore
- **Request a free street tree** in front of your home: https://www.treebaltimore.org/street-tree-request-form
- **Get a free tree** (see all options): https://www.treebaltimore.org/get-a-free-tree
- **Adopt a free yard tree** at spring and fall events: https://www.treebaltimore.org/shop
- **Plant trees yourself on public land** with trees from TreeBaltimore: https://www.treebaltimore.org/tree-order
- **Train as a TreeKeeper** (free tree planting and pruning certification): https://www.treebaltimore.org/treekeepers
- **Find tree events** like plantings and classes: https://www.treebaltimore.org/treeevents
- **See all programs** (TreeKeepers, Weed Warriors, Camp Small, Notable Trees): https://www.treebaltimore.org/programs
- **Donate**: https://www.treebaltimore.org/donate
- **Check the approved street tree list**: https://www.treebaltimore.org/approved-street-tree-list

## Contact
The TreeBaltimore home page lists treebaltimore@baltimorecity.gov and 410.396.6109 (and 6110) as contacts.

## Related city services
The city Forestry Division's FAQ (https://www.baltimorecity.gov/bcrp/baltimore-city-forestry-tree-services/faq) says problems with an existing street tree, such as a dead tree or one that needs pruning, should be reported to Baltimore 311.

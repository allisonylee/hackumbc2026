**Key takeaway:** TreeKeepers is TreeBaltimore's free, citywide training program that teaches residents how to plant and care for trees. It is open to all residents interested in helping Baltimore's trees, and you can earn a Planter or Pruner certification.

## Two certifications
**TreeKeeper Planter**
- 1 indoor class ("TK Introduction to Tree Planting")
- 1 outdoor field session ("TK Tree Planting Field Session")
- 2 volunteer tree planting events hosted by approved partners

**TreeKeeper Pruner**
- 1 combined indoor and outdoor class ("TK Introduction to Tree Pruning & Field Session")
- 2 volunteer tree pruning events hosted by approved partners

## Extra classes
"TreeKeepers Plus" classes cover topics like choosing trees, managing projects, tree names, and trees and the law. They are for learning and do not count toward certification.

## Why it matters
Certified TreeKeeper Planters qualify to plant and mulch street and park trees, and Pruners qualify to prune them. TreeBaltimore's free trees for public land (https://www.treebaltimore.org/tree-order) require a certified TreeKeeper or ISA certified arborist to be involved in planning and supervising the planting.

## How to sign up
1. See the upcoming class list: https://www.treebaltimore.org/treekeepers-upcoming-classes
2. Register for an intro class, then attend the field session and volunteer events.
3. Upcoming classes and plantings also appear on the TreeBaltimore events calendar: https://www.treebaltimore.org/treeevents

TreeBaltimore's other volunteer programs, like Baltimore Weed Warriors (invasive plant removal in parks), are listed at https://www.treebaltimore.org/programs.

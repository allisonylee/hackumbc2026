**Key takeaway:** You can support Baltimore's trees with money as well as time. Here are the official donation pages for four local groups.

## TreeBaltimore (city program)
TreeBaltimore's donate page (https://www.treebaltimore.org/donate) offers two options:
- **General TreeBaltimore fund**, for planting, caring for and protecting the city's trees: https://secure.qgiv.com/for/treebaltimore/
- **Camp Small**, the city's urban wood recycling facility, which turns debris from city street and park trees into lumber, firewood, furniture and other products: https://secure.qgiv.com/for/campsmall

## Baltimore Tree Trust
Donate page: https://www.baltimoretreetrust.org/ways-to-take-action/donate/
The page says donors help it plant over 2,000 trees and maintain over 6,500 trees a year, across more than 75 neighborhoods. It is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, so gifts are tax-deductible as allowed by law. For the cost of planting one street tree, see the separate document on street tree costs.

## Blue Water Baltimore
Donate page: https://bluewaterbaltimore.org/donate/
Offers one-time and recurring gifts online, plus gifts of stock, IRA distributions, vehicle donations and planned gifts. Donations support its clean water work.

## Parks & People Foundation
Donate page: https://parksandpeople.org/donate
Offers one-time gifts, monthly giving from $5 a month, workplace giving, stock gifts and planned giving. The page says gifts of $500 or more may qualify for a Maryland tax credit.

## Other ways to help
If you can't give money, you can volunteer at plantings, water a new street tree near you, or request a free street tree at https://www.treebaltimore.org/street-tree-request-form.

**Key takeaway:** New street trees need lots of water in their first few years. Baltimore Tree Trust says a new tree needs about **20 gallons of water each week from May to October** while it gets established, which takes about 2 to 3 years.

## Who takes care of a new tree
- For trees Baltimore Tree Trust plants, its Neighborhood Foresters maintain them for the **first two years** after planting.
- After that, the Baltimore City Department of Recreation and Parks' Forestry Division takes over.
- Baltimore Tree Trust says it will replace any tree that dies within its **two-year warranty**.

Even with this care, the FAQ says you can help a nearby tree by watering it yourself, especially during a severe drought or if its leaves are browning, wilting or curling.

## How you can help a new tree
1. **Water it:** about 20 gallons per week from May through October. Water slowly so it soaks in.
2. **Mulch it:** a ring of natural, undyed mulch keeps the soil moist. Keep mulch pulled back from the trunk.
3. **Protect it:** keep the tree well free of litter, and loosen anything tied around the trunk before it gets too tight.
4. **Report problems:** if a tree Baltimore Tree Trust planted looks dead or unhealthy, email info@baltimoretreetrust.org. For stump removal and other city requests, the FAQ says to call 311.

## How trees are chosen
Baltimore Tree Trust follows "right tree, right place," choosing city-approved species that fit each spot. Standard tree wells are 4 by 8 feet or larger.

## How neighborhoods get trees
Baltimore Tree Trust reaches out to neighborhoods through local groups, mailers and door-knocking before a planting project, and residents can give input. You can also request a street tree from the city at https://www.treebaltimore.org/street-tree-request-form.

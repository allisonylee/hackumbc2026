**Key takeaway:** Parks & People Foundation is a Baltimore nonprofit focused on green spaces, parks and environmental education. It offers volunteer roles in its community garden, youth programs and events. Its volunteer page does not list tree-planting events specifically.

## Volunteer options
- **Community garden:** planting, weeding and harvesting.
- **Youth programs:** helping at SuperKids Camp, as a Counselor-in-Training, Environmental Educator Assistant or Administrative Assistant.
- **Community events:** such as the annual Easter Egg Hunt and Picnic in the Park.
- **Corporate groups:** team volunteer days.
- **Internships:** in communications, park projects or youth programs.

## How to sign up
1. Go to https://parksandpeople.org/volunteer.
2. Fill out the online volunteer application linked on that page, or email volunteer@parksandpeople.org.
3. For internships, send a resume to volunteer@parksandpeople.org.

## Donate
Parks & People accepts donations at https://parksandpeople.org/donate. Options include one-time gifts, monthly giving (starting at $5 a month), stock gifts and planned giving. The page says gifts of $500 or more may qualify for a Maryland tax credit.

## Looking for tree planting?
For hands-on tree planting, try TreeBaltimore events (https://www.treebaltimore.org/treeevents), Baltimore Tree Trust (https://www.baltimoretreetrust.org/ways-to-take-action/volunteer/) or Blue Water Baltimore (https://bluewaterbaltimore.org/volunteer-opportunities-baltimore/).

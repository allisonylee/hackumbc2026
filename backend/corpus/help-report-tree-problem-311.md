**Key takeaway:** To report a dead street tree, a tree that needs pruning, or a fallen tree or branch in Baltimore City, contact **311**. The city's Forestry Division handles street tree maintenance.

## How to contact 311
- Call **311** from inside Baltimore City.
- Call **(443) 263-2220** from outside the city.
- Or make a request online at the Baltimore 311 portal: https://balt311.baltimorecity.gov/

## What to report
- **Dead tree:** The FAQ says a street tree counts as dead when all of its leaves have turned brown. Newly planted trees can drop leaves from transplant shock and may leaf out again within about six weeks, so wait before reporting a brand-new tree.
- **Partly dead tree:** If more than one-third of the branches are dead, ask for an inspection by city forestry staff.
- **Pruning:** Report a street tree that needs pruning. The FAQ says trees usually only need pruning every five to seven years, mostly to lift branches so people can walk on the sidewalk.
- **Fallen trees or branches:** A tree or part of a tree blocking a road or fallen on private property, such as a car or house, is an emergency and gets an immediate response. A tree blocking a sidewalk is not an emergency but should still be reported to 311.
- **Sidewalk lifted by roots:** Ask 311 for the Footways Division to repair the sidewalk. The city says it will not remove a tree just because of a sidewalk conflict.

## Removing a street tree
The FAQ says removal permits are only issued when needed to repair a leaking sewer, because mature trees give so many benefits.

## Who does the work
The FAQ says the Forestry Division maintains the city's street trees using four in-house crews and nine contract crews.

## Want a new tree instead?
To request a new street tree, use https://www.treebaltimore.org/street-tree-request-form.

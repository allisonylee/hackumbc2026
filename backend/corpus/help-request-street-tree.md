title: "How do I request a street tree for my street in Baltimore?"

---

**Key takeaway:** Baltimore City residents and property owners can ask the city to plant a street tree in front of their home by filling out TreeBaltimore's Street Tree Request form. The page does not list any cost.

## Who can request
Baltimore City residents and property owners.

## Steps
1. Go to https://www.treebaltimore.org/street-tree-request-form and fill out the request form.
2. If there is no tree pit in the sidewalk, you can use the same form to ask for a new pit to be created.
3. After you submit, you get an email with a link you can use to check your request's status.

## What happens next
1. A city inspector visits and checks the location and what site preparation is needed.
2. If approved, any prep work is done.
3. The site is given to a planting contractor for the next planting season.

Planting seasons are **fall (mid-October to mid-December)** and **spring (mid-March to mid-May)**. The page says you may wait one or two seasons before your tree is planted.

## If you want to plant a street tree yourself
- **Existing tree pit:** you need a Forestry permit. Get it by making a 311 request for "Street Tree Inspection" (https://balt311.baltimorecity.gov/).
- **New tree pit (cutting sidewalk):** you need permits from both the Department of Transportation's Right of Way office and Forestry, and you must call Miss Utility (1-800-257-7777) to mark underground utilities first.

The approved species list is at https://www.treebaltimore.org/approved-street-tree-list.

## Questions
The page lists treesplease@baltimorecity.gov for questions.

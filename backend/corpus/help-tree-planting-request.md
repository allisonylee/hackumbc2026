**Key takeaway:** Baltimore City residents and property owners can get free trees from TreeBaltimore to plant themselves on public parkland or along city streets, as long as a certified TreeKeeper or ISA certified arborist is involved in planning and supervising the planting.

## Who it is for
Baltimore City residents and property owners who want permission to plant trees themselves on Recreation and Parks parkland or in the city's public right-of-way.

## Requirements
- A **certified TreeKeeper or ISA certified arborist** must supervise the planting. (You can become a TreeKeeper through free training: https://www.treebaltimore.org/treekeepers.)
- You must commit to **water and care for the trees for 2 years**. After that, the city's Forestry Division takes over maintenance.
- Street tree proposals must include an address for each tree. If you create new tree pits in the right-of-way, you also need a permit from the Department of Transportation's right-of-way office.

## Deadlines
- Spring planting: request by **January 15**. Trees usually arrive by April 15.
- Fall planting: request by **June 15**. Trees usually arrive by September 15.

## Steps
1. Go to https://www.treebaltimore.org/tree-order and submit the Tree Planting Request form before the deadline.
2. Choose delivery or pickup at the Druid Hill Nursery.
3. Ask for extras if you need them: stakes, ties, deer protection and mulch are available on request.
4. Plant with your certified supervisor, then water and maintain the trees for two years.

If you just want one tree in your own yard, the yard tree adoption events are simpler: https://www.treebaltimore.org/shop.

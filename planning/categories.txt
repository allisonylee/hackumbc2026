# Club Guilds
🎮 **Game Jamathon**, founded by **Game Developers Club**
-# Game Jam - Want to make a game? Write code, make art, compose music, design mechanics, and build stories to create a playable game for our Game Jamathon! Submissions should be functional, have good aesthetics, be polished, creative, and have a strong connection to our theme announced at the opening ceremony. Projects will be judged by presenting them to our industry professional judges during the judging period. As for what you make, there are no restrictions on what kind of game to make. However, any AI usage must be disclosed, and you may not use AI for asset creation.

👾 **Cybersecurity Application Track**, founded by **CyberDawgs**
-# Cyber Track - Your challenge is to develop a sophisticated application that assists in the overall goal of increasing the Cybersecurity capabilities of defenders whether that be in logging, hardening or otherwise. In addition to this app you must provide extensive documentation on how the app works, what it does and what users have control over. 

🏥 **Health Beyond the Clinic: The Future of Care**, founded by **Premed Society**
-# Health Track - You will identify a real problem involving healthcare, public health, accessibility, hospital systems, wellness, fitness, mental health, or health education and build a technological solution for it. 

🍃 **Best Environmental Idea**, founded by **ETF**
-# Environmental Track - Design a project that reduces environmental harm or strengthens ecological resilience by critically and creatively applying artificial intelligence. Your submission should explore how technology can be used responsibly, ethically, and sustainably and how to put the Earth/climate/environment/local community habitats first while acknowledging the real environmental costs of AI systems. 

🤝 **Community Impact and Social Innovation**, founded by **STARS**
-# Social Impact Track - Design a project that demonstrates a clear understanding of the needs of the Baltimore or UMBC community and explain how the proposed solution could create meaningful, measurable impact. Teams should present a realistic plan for evaluating that impact and show how their project responds to a genuine community challenge.

💻 **Best Entrepreneurial Idea**, founded by **Entrepreneurs Club**
-# Best Entrepreneurial Idea - Pick a real-world problem in any field, and design a product or service that solves it. This isn't limited to any one industry: your idea could be a physical product, a digital platform, a service, or anything in between. The goal is to walk away with a working demo and a pitch that could convince real users and real investors that your idea is worth backing.

# Company and Miscellaneous Guilds
📜 **Navigating the Future: Career Pathways & Degree ROI**, founded by **Division of Information Technology**
-# DoIT Track - Every student asks themselves: "Where will my degree actually take me?" In this track, teams will use rich synthetic campus and alumni datasets—spanning majors, graduation years, industry movements, salary bands, micro-credentials, and course records—to build interactive tools that help students chart their academic and professional journeys. Whether you design a visual career-pathway graph, an AI advisor that calculates degree ROI, a tool that matches student engagement to real-world job outcomes or something else, your goal is to help students translate their time at UMBC into long-term success.

💵 **NextGen Finance Innovation Challenge**, founded by **T. Rowe Price**
-# T. Rowe Price Track - The financial industry is being reshaped by exchange-traded funds (ETFs), artificial intelligence (AI), and digital assets and tokenization. This challenge asks teams to identify a real financial problem and show how one or more of these technologies could be used to solve it responsibly.

🖼️ **Best Design**, founded by **hackUMBC**
-# Best Design - Your task is simple: using the reference photo as a baseline, showcase your finest art skills to get as close as possible to immortalizing True Grit's pure reverence into a piece of media. The medium you work in is up to you; whether you choose to photoshop the baseline photo or draft an animated GIF, Her Truest Grit will accept any and all forms of submission.

👑 **Best Meme**, founded by **hackUMBC**
-# Best Meme - Take a photo of an organizer (or group of organizers) from the google drive and turn them into a meme! Upload your meme to ⁠best-meme-track whenever you're ready. All community members will vote on the best one!
